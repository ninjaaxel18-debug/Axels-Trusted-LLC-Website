Clear planning before work begins

A good bathroom project starts with clear expectations. During the estimate, discuss the existing room, preferred materials, access and any dependencies that may affect timing. The written scope should identify what Axels Trusted LLC will provide and what is outside that scope.

Help us understand your project

  1. Send wide photos of the bathroom plus close-ups of problem areas.
  2. Describe the look or function you want to improve.
  3. Mention any materials you have already purchased or selected.

If the project requires separately licensed trade work, confirm how that portion will be handled before contracting.
