Describe the damage before planning the repair

Include where the damage is located, roughly how large the area is and whether the underlying cause has been resolved. A small damaged patch and a larger replacement area can require different work. If moisture or another ongoing problem caused the damage, discuss it before planning a cosmetic repair so the estimate addresses the condition you are seeing.

Discuss the existing texture and finish

Texture repairs depend on the existing surface and finish. Send a close-up photo of the texture and a wider photo showing the repair area when you contact Axel. We can discuss the intended finish and whether painting or additional preparation should be part of the scope. An exact texture match should not be assumed without assessing the wall.

Smaller jobs are welcome

You do not need a full renovation to ask for help. Drywall repair, texture repair and related home improvements can be discussed as individual projects. Include all areas you want assessed so the estimate covers your request clearly.

Common questions

Do I need to know the name of my wall texture?

No. Describe the finish and have photos ready. The existing surface can be discussed when assessing the repair.

Can I request painting along with the repair?

Yes. Interior painting is also offered. Mention it in your request so the preparation and painting scope can be discussed.
